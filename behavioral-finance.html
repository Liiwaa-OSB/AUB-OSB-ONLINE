THE CERTIFICATE

The Online Professional Certificate in Behavioral Finance at the Suliman S. Olayan School of Business, American University of Beirut, is designed as a focused professional credential for developing expertise in the psychological and behavioral factors that influence financial decision-making.

The certificate targets a broad range of professionals and graduates interested in understanding how cognitive limitations, emotions, and social influences shape financial decisions and market behavior. It is designed for individuals across finance, investment, business, consulting, and other fields seeking to develop a deeper understanding of the behavioral dimensions of finance.

The certificate emphasizes the intersection of psychology, economics, and finance, covering areas such as prospect theory, loss aversion, overconfidence, herd behavior, mental accounting, and other behavioral biases. Through interactive online lectures and real-world case studies, participants will develop the ability to identify and evaluate behavioral biases, analyze their impact on financial decisions and market dynamics, and apply behavioral insights to investment strategies, corporate finance, and personal financial decisions.

This certificate provides a focused learning experience for professionals aiming to strengthen their financial decisionmaking, broaden their understanding of investment and market behavior, or develop specialized knowledge in behavioral finance.

WHY THIS CERTIFICATE

PSYCHOLOGY MEETS FINANCE
Understand the cognitive and emotional factors that shape financial decisions.

Explore how psychological biases, emotions, and social influences affect investors, markets, and financial decision-making. Examine behavioral concepts such as loss aversion, overconfidence, herd behavior, and mental accounting, and understand how they challenge traditional assumptions about rational financial behavior.

PRACTICAL & REALWORLD
Apply behavioral finance concepts to actual financial decisions and market situations.

Go beyond theoretical concepts through real-world case studies and practical applications. Examine how behavioral insights can inform investment management, portfolio construction, risk management, corporate finance, and personal financial decisions, with attention to relevant Middle East examples.

SHORT, EFFECTIVE & FLEXIBLE
A focused 2 month learning experience designed for working professionals.

Develop a comprehensive understanding of behavioral finance through a focused and flexible online format. Learn at your own pace through asynchronous content, while benefiting from interactive sessions and opportunities for engagement with professors and peers.

Certificate Instructor

Assem Safieddine

Finance, Accounting & Managerial Economics

Dr. Safieddine holds a Ph.D. in Finance from Boston College. His research has been published in leading international finance journals, widely cited by renowned scholars, and featured in prominent academic and business publications. His expertise includes risk management, capital acquisition, mergers and acquisitions, cost of capital, and corporate governance.
Dr. Safieddine has held academic appointments at Michigan State University, Harvard University Summer School, and the American University of Beirut (AUB), where he has served since 2003. At AUB, he held leadership roles including Associate Dean of Academic Programs and Faculty Affairs and Chair of the Finance, Accounting, and Managerial Economics Track. He is a sought-after speaker on finance topics and has advised leading international organizations, government entities, and multinational corporations on corporate governance and financial restructuring.

WHO SHOULD ENROLL?

The certificate is open to learners from diverse academic and professional backgrounds who are interested in understanding the behavioral factors that influence financial decisions and market behavior.

It is particularly relevant for:
  1. Finance and investment professionals seeking to strengthen their understanding of investor behavior and decision-making.
  2. Business and finance graduates looking to develop specialized knowledge in behavioral finance.
  3. Consultants and advisors involved in financial strategies, market decisions, or client decision-making.
  4. Professionals with an interest in psychology and human behavior who want to explore its application to finance.
  5. Entrepreneurs and business owners interested in improving financial decisionmaking.
  6. Professionals considering a transition into finance or seeking to broaden their understanding of financial markets.
