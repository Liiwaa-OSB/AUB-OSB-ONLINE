The Diploma

The Online Professional Graduate Diploma in Risk and Regulation is a 12-credit graduate professional diploma offered by the American University of Beirut (AUB) through the Suliman S. Olayan School of Business (OSB) and endorsed by the Banking Control Commission of Lebanon (BCCL). The program is designed to equip professionals with the knowledge and applied skills needed to interpret, implement, and align financial regulatory frameworks with institutional risk management, governance, compliance, and supervisory practices.

Delivered fully online through an interactive and flexible learning model, the diploma combines live synchronous sessions with asynchronous learning activities to accommodate working professionals. The curriculum integrates academic rigor with regulatory and industry perspectives and emphasizes practical application through case studies, scenario-based discussions, regulatory simulations, group exercises, and applied institutional analysis.

Participants complete three core courses in banking risk and risk governance, global capital standards, and global liquidity standards, followed by one specialization course in either Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT) or Operational Resilience, Recovery & Resolution. Participants may also complete the remaining specialization course through an optional extended pathway.

WHO SHOULD ENROLL?

The diploma is designed for professionals seeking to strengthen their expertise in banking regulation, risk management, compliance, governance, and financial supervision.

You are:
  1. Central bankers, financial regulators, financial controllers, bank supervisors, and supervisory authority professionals
  2. Banking professionals, particularly risk and compliance officers
  3. Internal and external auditors
  4. Professionals working in AML/CFT and financial crime compliance
  5. Consultants advising organizations on financial strategy, capital structure, compliance, and regulatory adherence
  6. Legal and legal-banking professionals
  7. Professionals in regulatory bodies, academia, and research
PROGRAM REQUIREMENTS
  • Applicants should normally hold a bachelor's degree from a recognized institution, preferably in business, finance, accounting, economics, engineering, management, or a related field.
  • Candidates from other disciplines may also be considered based on relevant professional or managerial experience.
  • Applicants must satisfy AUB's applicable English proficiency requirements.
  • Changes, exemptions, and exceptions are subject to the University's latest policies.
